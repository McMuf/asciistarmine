ASCIISTARMINE

You signed a contract you didn't finish reading, for a company that mines the belt for whatever's underneath it. Fourteen months are missing from your memory. The rig still owes the Company more than you'll ever mine back.

BEFORE YOU START

  • STORES (left) are your resources. Power and oxygen drain constantly. Keep them above zero.
  • ACTIONS (center) are things you can do. Some cost resources, most have a cooldown before you can use them again.
  • LOG (below actions) records everything that's happened, most recent on top.
  • When a box pops up over everything else, that's an event. Read it and pick a choice. Choices matter and some come back later.
  • REPUTATION and COMPANIONS (right) fill in as you meet people and make choices. The CODEX button appears there once you've found something worth reading.
